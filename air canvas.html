🚀 Ultimate Air Draw App

Instructions

  • ✍️ Hold Z to draw in the air.
  • 🤏 Pinch thumb and index finger to select a drawing.
  • 🖐️ Open your hand to use the duster and erase nearby drawings.
  • 🗑️ Hold D and pinch to delete a selected drawing.
  • 📦 Pinch and drag to move a selected drawing.
  • ⌨️ Press Space to clear everything.
  • 📷 Allow camera access when prompted.
